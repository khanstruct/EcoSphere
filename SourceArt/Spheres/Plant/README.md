## Surface prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a production game texture for EcoSphere's Plant Life elemental sphere. A FLAT full-bleed 2:1 equirectangular color/albedo texture, NOT a rendered globe. Dense overlapping leafy foliage, broad readable small and medium leaves in rich emerald, forest green and fresh lime green, clear veins and soft painterly faceted detail. Match the polished colorful hand-painted material style of a fantasy elemental bubble shooter. Leafy organic canopy, no flowers, fruit, soil or large branches. Even diffuse illumination, no central hotspot or spherical shading, no vignette or empty margins. Cover every pixel with foliage. Seamless left/right wrapping for a sphere, quieter detail near top/bottom. Broad forms must remain readable when exported at 256x128 pixels. No text, no border, no icon overlay, no transparency. Landscape aspect ratio exactly 2:1.

Create a NEW matching PLANT LIFE icon: a single bold broad green leaf with a short connected stem, pointed tip angled gently upper right, strong simple silhouette. One clean pale central vein with a few simple angled side veins integrated into the faceted design.
